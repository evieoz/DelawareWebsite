WELCOME TO NEWARK

A college town.
A connected campus.

The University of Delaware is located in Newark, Delaware, where a classic campus setting meets an active college-town atmosphere.

Red-brick buildings, tree-lined walkways and gathering spaces give campus its recognizable character, while classrooms, research spaces and student centers support everyday college life.

A UNIVERSITY WITH HISTORY

Since 1743.

UD's history reaches back to 1743. Today, the university combines that long tradition with modern academic, research and student opportunities.
